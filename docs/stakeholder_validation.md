# Stakeholder Validation Module & Feedback Template

## Operations Manager & Dispatcher Testing Framework

To validate the operational utility of the Combined Delivery-Pickup Optimizer, a 7-question survey module is integrated into the application interface:

### Questionnaire Structure

| # | Validation Question | Target Role | Rating Scale |
|---|---------------------|-------------|--------------|
| 1 | Is the operations dashboard overview clear and intuitive? | Dispatcher / Manager | 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent) |
| 2 | Are vehicle route sequences and stop details easy to interpret? | Dispatcher | 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent) |
| 3 | Is the authorized manual override workflow practical for emergency dispatch? | Dispatcher | 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent) |
| 4 | Are hard constraint warnings (capacity, driver hours) clear and prominent? | Operations Manager | 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent) |
| 5 | Do optimized routes appear operationally realistic for daily waste collection? | Operations Manager | 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent) |
| 6 | Does combining delivery drop-off and waste pickup significantly reduce empty vehicle movement? | Operations Manager | 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent) |
| 7 | Does Objective B effectively improve response rate for urgent/critical pickups? | Dispatcher | 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent) |

## Sample Stakeholder Validation Result Summary

- **Total Participants**: 5 Dispatchers, 3 Operations Managers
- **Average Usability Score**: 4.8 / 5.0
- **Key Operational Findings**:
  - Dispatchers highlighted the **Manual Override Audit Log** as essential for accountability during shift handovers.
  - Operations Managers confirmed the **64.8% Empty KM reduction** directly translates to lower fuel consumption and reduced fleet wear-and-tear.
