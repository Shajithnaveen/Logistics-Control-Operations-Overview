Prototype System Limitations & Operational Error Analysis

System Limitations Document

  • Synthetic Geo-Dataset: Coordinates and demand figures generated synthetically around Metro central grid.
  • Travel Time Model: Uses Manhattan distance approximation with constant 30 km/h average speed.
  • Dynamic Traffic: Real-time road congestion and construction delays not integrated in prototype phase.
  • Waste Compaction: Fixed density waste weight model; volume expansion non-modeled.

Pending Request Error Breakdown

  • Capacity Exceeded: 8 requests pending due to max vehicle payload limits on 4,000 kg light fleet units.
  • Tight Service Windows: 4 pickups unassigned because arrival time exceeded 17:00 shift limit.
  • Driver Workload Protection: 6 assignments rejected to prevent driver shift exceeding max work limit hours.
